---
title: Music Remote
description: Control the Music app on another Mac from your menu bar.
---

Music Remote puts the Music app of another Mac in your menu bar: a Mac mini on the stereo, played
from your MacBook as if it were the MacBook's own Music.

## What you can do

- **Now Playing**: artwork, the current track, progress, play and pause, skip, shuffle, repeat,
  volume and love, with your Mac's media keys and Control Center.
- **Search** your library by title, artist or album.
- **Browse** playlists and folders as in Music's sidebar, an album grid with covers, and artists.
- **Up Next**: queue songs and albums to play next.
- **Speakers**: play to AirPlay speakers, several at once, each with its own volume.
- **Yamaha receivers**: power, input, sound mode, mute and volume, right next to the music.

## Set up

Install Music Remote on both Macs. On the Mac that plays the music, open
**Settings → This Mac** and turn on **Let other Macs control Music on this Mac**. Every other Mac with
the app finds it on your network by itself: no pairing codes.

## Install

- **Homebrew**: `brew install --cask goudsmit-tech/tap/music-remote`
- **Download** the DMG, open it and drag Music Remote to Applications.

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
